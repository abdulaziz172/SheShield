💡 Safety Tips

  • Share your live location with a trusted contact when traveling alone.
  • Keep your phone charged and easily accessible at all times.
  • Avoid poorly lit or isolated areas, especially at night.
  • Trust your instincts — if a situation feels unsafe, leave immediately.
  • Save local emergency numbers (police: 100, women's helpline: 1091) in your phone.
  • Vary your daily routine and routes when possible.
  • Inform someone you trust about your travel plans and expected arrival time.
  • Keep a whistle or personal safety alarm accessible.

ℹ️ About SheShield

SheShield is a smart women's safety web app built to provide quick access to emergency help, live location sharing, nearby police stations, and incident reporting — all in one place.

In an emergency, always call your local emergency number first (India: 100 for police, 1091 for women's helpline).
